Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Baron: Impulse Buy Blocker is operated by Bulbul Labs LLC. We don't sell your data or feed it into secret algorithms. The app has one job: helping you block impulse purchases.

What We Collect

  • Scan history and images: The cart screenshots you scan and their analysis results (product name, price, Baron's verdict). This is kept only to run the app and to show you your own history.
  • Profile information: The anonymous profile you create when you first open the app (your dream goal, spending habits, and a rough figure for what a month of non-essentials costs you). You are never asked for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): You can save your account with your Apple ID so a new phone or a reinstall brings it back. We ask Apple for no name and no email — only the private identifier Apple issues for Baron, which is linked to your account so you can sign back in.
  • Subscription status: Whether you're a Pro member and when that access ends. (Payment details are handled securely by Apple/Google and never reach our servers.)
  • Usage events: Five anonymous events — starting and finishing onboarding, completing a scan, opening the paywall, and making a purchase — tied to the same anonymous ID as your profile.

Who Else Sees It

Running the app means some of your data leaves our servers. These are the only places it goes, and none of them are advertisers:

  • The AI that reads your cart. When you check something, the screenshot or photo (or the item you type in) is sent to a third-party AI model provider, together with your dream goal, your spending habits, your monthly non-essentials figure, your roast tone and your last ten checks, and it returns the verdict and the roast. No name, email or contacts are sent. This is what makes the app work; there is no version of the app that analyses your cart without it. The provider processes the image to answer that one request.
  • Our hosting provider. Your profile, scan history and the images themselves are stored with Supabase, which runs our database and file storage.
  • Our analytics provider. The five usage events above go to PostHog, hosted in the EU.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple. Apple confirms who you are and gives us the identifier above; nothing else.
  • The app stores. Apple and Google handle payment and tell us only whether a subscription is active.

How Your Data Is Used

Everything we collect is used only to give you a personalized roast and to keep your Vault statistics. It is never sold, and it is never shared with third-party advertisers.

Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account, along with all of your scan history and images, at any time from Settings → "Delete account." This removes your rows from our database; it cannot reach copies already processed by the providers listed above.
