Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Curb, we don't sell your data or feed it into secret algorithms. The app has one job: helping you block impulse purchases.

What We Collect

  • Scan history and images: The cart screenshots you scan and their analysis results (product name, price, Baron's verdict). This is kept only to run the app and to show you your own history.
  • Profile information: The anonymous profile you create when you first open the app (your dream goal, spending habits). You are never asked for your name, email address or phone number, and the app has no sign-up screen.
  • Subscription status: Whether you're a Pro member and when that access ends. (Payment details are handled securely by Apple/Google and never reach our servers.)
  • Usage events: Five anonymous events — starting and finishing onboarding, completing a scan, opening the paywall, and making a purchase — tied to the same anonymous ID as your profile.

Who Else Sees It

Running the app means some of your data leaves our servers. These are the only places it goes, and none of them are advertisers:

  • The AI that reads your cart. When you scan, the screenshot is sent to a third-party AI model provider, which returns the verdict and the roast. This is what makes the app work; there is no version of Curb that analyses your cart without it. The provider processes the image to answer that one request.
  • Our hosting provider. Your profile, scan history and the images themselves are stored with Supabase, which runs our database and file storage.
  • Our analytics provider. The five usage events above go to PostHog, hosted in the EU.
  • The app stores. Apple and Google handle payment and tell us only whether a subscription is active.

How Your Data Is Used

Everything we collect is used only to give you a personalized roast and to keep your Vault statistics. It is never sold, and it is never shared with third-party advertisers.

Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account, along with all of your scan history and images, at any time from Settings → "Delete My Account." This removes your rows from our database; it cannot reach copies already processed by the providers listed above.
